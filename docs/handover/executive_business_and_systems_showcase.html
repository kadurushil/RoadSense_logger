Core Embedded & Control Systems Breakthroughs

Solving timing, serialization, dynamic photometry, and vehicle bus integration

⏱️

1. Deterministic Multi-Modal Time Synchronization

Eliminating spatial parallax error without bulky hardware Pulse-Per-Second (PPS) cables

The Physical Challenge: At 100 km/h (27.8 m/s), an uncalibrated 30 ms clock jitter displaces radar targets by 0.83 meters from the optical camera image.
The RoadSense Solution: Anchored to ARM CPU invariant monotonic counter (CNTVCT_EL0) latched at driver interrupt boundaries. Common timeline shared in session_timeline.csv.
⚡

2. Zero-Drop 3.125 Mbps High-Speed Serial Ingestion

Sustaining ~312.5 KB/sec continuous binary stream with zero FIFO buffer overruns

● ZERO DROPPED BYTES
The Embedded Challenge: CP2105 USB UART FIFO holds only 576 bytes. A 1.8 ms CPU stall overflows the buffer, causing byte loss and corrupted tracking.
The RoadSense Solution: Elevated native polling worker (NORM_PRIORITY+1) with a 64 KB direct ring buffer and self-indexing 24-byte binary framing headers.
👁️

3. Dynamic Automotive Photometry (Autonomous Road AE)

Closed-loop dual-zone photometric feedback to prevent sky blowout blinding road targets

❌ Standard Matrix Auto-Exposure
BRIGHT SKY ZONE (High Luminance)
ROAD SURFACE (Pitched Black / Clipped)

Camera darkens the entire frame; vehicle chassis & road tarmac disappear.

✅ RoadSense Dual-Zone Photometric AE
SKY BLOOM (Intentionally Clipped)
ROAD SURFACE (Optimal Mid-Grey Target EV)

Samples 32x24 luminance grid; applies +1.0 to +3.0 EV bias to lock road exposure.
